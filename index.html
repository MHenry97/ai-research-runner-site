AI Research Runner

AI Research Runner is a privately operated desktop application being developed to help its owner organize and carry out research assignments.

How it is intended to work

The application is designed to receive research instructions through a dedicated Gmail account, coordinate research tasks on an owner-controlled computer, and send progress updates or completed reports back by email. Research outputs may be stored in a private GitHub repository.

Access and privacy

This is a personal-use application, not a public research service. Gmail authorization is intended only for the owner's designated account. The public website does not display emails, research assignments, credentials, or private repository contents.

A separate privacy policy page will describe the application's intended handling of Google account data.
